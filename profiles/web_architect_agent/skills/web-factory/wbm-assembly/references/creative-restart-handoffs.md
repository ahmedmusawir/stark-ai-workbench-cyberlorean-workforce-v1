# Creative restart and design-to-build handoffs

## Reusable workflow

1. Recover approved pages/routes from current brief, rulings and actual source evidence. Distinguish source URL from destination route. Do not ask the Director to repeat recorded decisions. Resolve stale counts against actual source headings: campaign formats and capabilities, for example, are different categories.
2. Record rejection and current authorization without erasing prior work. A selected concept, full-design approval, engineering release and publication are separate decisions. Keep obsolete WBM contracts clearly non-executable until reconciliation; do not claim final files exist before delivery.
3. Actually render the selected references and inspect artwork/assets. Read source content for meaningful service detail rather than relying on earlier summaries. Contact sheets can survey all images efficiently, but small text requires full-resolution inspection or source text. Do not describe unreadable details as observed.
4. Turn references into qualities, not pasted sections. Editorial layering, expressive typography, sculptural imagery, flowing boundaries and dark/light rhythm may inform one coherent direction. Preserve original identity and unchanged logo without forcing identical colour distribution on every section.
5. Require an imagery plan before implementation. Inspect existing imagery first; distinguish downloaded files, screenshot depictions and URL-only references. Neither availability nor a screenshot establishes rights. Locally delivered assets may be sourced or generated where tools and rights permit. Record provenance; never suggest concept images depict actual staff, premises, client work or results.
6. Allow design-authored headlines, transitions and illustrations that preserve supported meaning. A missing verbatim source is not automatically an invented business claim. Trace the underlying meaning; flag new factual assertions separately. Keep process notes outside marketing copy except explicitly required notices.
7. For a restart caused by repetitive pages, commission complete Home plus one source-rich service page, not another entire site. Include all lower sections/footer. Require shared identity but meaningful differences in composition and visual content. Preserve the eventual approved page map.
8. Require working responsive prototypes, local assets/fonts, desktop/mobile full-page screenshots, a large-preview index, imagery rationale, source notes, and separate visual-review/technical-preflight results. Designer renders, inspects, refines and compares the pages side by side. Director approves creative direction; tests do not.
9. When a fresh conversation is requested, route the work through a new Designer conversation and explicit reference paths. Preserve previous sessions; do not reset/delete or contact peers without authority. Let the operator coordinate session-separation verification.
10. After final design review, reconcile actual prototype/screenshot/asset paths, content model, acceptance criteria, mirrored gates and embedded QAM. Refresh Developer planning before implementation authorization. Intended model names are not observable runtime identity. Image-tool availability in the Architect runtime does not prove Developer capability; arrange an actual image-open check at the next authorized idle/resume boundary.

## Research honesty

Use a small selection of relevant agency/design references; record URLs and specific visually observed qualities. Direct browsing can supplement unavailable search. Distinguish a loaded page, a partially rendered page, and a fully inspected page. Missing video or lazy-loaded regions limit what can be claimed. Never treat failed asset retrieval as evidence that an asset is unsuitable or unlicensed; name availability and rights gaps separately.

## Case-derived caution

A direction with expressive serif type and folded artwork became repetitive when expanded through minor object variations and text rows. The lesson is not to ban those ingredients: evaluate page-specific storytelling and sustained lower-page visual quality before scaling. A source-rich PPC page offered planning/approval/launch/optimization material, named capabilities and campaign formats suitable for purposeful visual explanations without fabricated metrics.
